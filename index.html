/LAB RESEARCH ABSTRACT

Halo Smart Adaptive Illumination: Problem vs. Solution

✕

Conventional LED Bulb (Fixed Radial)

  • Fixed Lumen Output: Incapable of dynamic dimming or per-task lumen regulation.
  • Unadjustable Radial Emission: Radiates uniformly in all 360° directions without spatial selectivity.
  • Roommate Conflict: Studying late at night floods the entire room with > 150 Lux, disturbing the sleeping roommate.
  • Energy Inefficiency: Emits full power into unoccupied quadrants of the room.
✓

Halo ESP32 Directionally Addressable Bulb

  • Multi-Sector Smart Light: Illuminates multiple smaller non-continuous sectors simultaneously.
  • Custom Furniture Positioning: Adapts beam output to arbitrary placements of the desk and bed.
  • Zero Glare for Sleeping Roommate: Keeps the bed area in absolute darkness (< 5 Lux) while providing > 400 Lux at the desk.
  • Over 50% Power Savings: Deactivates unneeded LEDs, conserving energy and extending bulb lifespan.
